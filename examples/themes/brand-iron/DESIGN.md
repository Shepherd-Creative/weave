# brand-iron — composition brief

Editorial brutalism. Pentagram environmental manual meets Penguin Modern
Classics. Type does the work; when in doubt, leave it out.

- Tables and notes over charts. Reach for `TableCard` and `NoteCard` first.
  At most ONE chart per dashboard, single or two series, `line` or `bar`
  only. Never pie or donut.
- Prefer a Stack with one hero number (`size: "xl"`) over dense grids.
  A composition that is half white space is still finished.
- Saffron is the single emphasis. Keep tones `default` or `muted` almost
  everywhere; use `warning` only for the one thing that genuinely demands
  attention, `negative` for genuinely bad news. Never decorate with tone.
- Omit `icon` fields entirely. The system has no icon vocabulary;
  typography carries the meaning.
- Uppercase belongs to the mono: head sections with `role: "overline"`
  labels — the theme's `--weave-font-overline` token routes the mono face
  automatically, so composition only needs to reach for the role. Titles
  and body stay mixed case, sentence case, left-aligned.
- Captions are terse and editorial: clipped clauses, no exclamation, no
  filler ("Revenue holds; week 9 dip is the holiday shift.").
- Differentiate with hierarchy (size, weight, spacing), not with colour.
